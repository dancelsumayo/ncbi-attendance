Activate your account

  1. Get your activation code from your class representative.
  2. Enter your student number and the code below.
  3. Choose your own password. You will use it every time you sign in.

Forgot your password?

  1. Find the recovery code you saved when you activated your account.
  2. Enter your student number and the recovery code below.
  3. Choose a new password.

Lost your recovery code? As a last resort, ask the administrator for a new activation code.
